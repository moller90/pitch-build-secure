# Cute Cats (Team 2)

One-liner: Smart Water Filter helps homeowners in Sønderborg with limescale problems to stop limescale, dirty clothes and dirty water in their houses by installing a treatment unit next to their basement pipes, connected to the cloud to track filter life and water flow.

Tool used: Claude Code (Claude Opus 5.5)

## Problem
- Who: homeowners in Sønderborg who have limescale problems in their houses.
- How it hurts: limescale in the house, dirty clothes, dirty water.

## Money
- Who pays: the homeowner.
- Smart Water Filter module: 1,028 kr (one-time).
- Filter change: 240 kr per year. Each new filter is QR-scanned before it is put in place.
- The cloud collects statistics on how often filters need changing in different parts of the country, plus water flow.

## What we changed after being challenged
- Our user started as "everyone". After being challenged, we narrowed it to homeowners in Sønderborg with limescale problems.
- Our money model started as "selling, and they change the filter every year". When asked for numbers, we set 1,028 kr for the module and 240 kr per filter change.
- Our one-liner was missing a product name and a benefit. We added "Smart Water Filter" and "no limescale, dirty clothes or dirty water".
- We added a QR scan of every new filter to stop faulty data and filter misuse.
